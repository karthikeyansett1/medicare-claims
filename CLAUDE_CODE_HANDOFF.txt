4. MEASURE INVARIANTS — DO NOT SILENTLY CHANGE
All identifiers and grouping keys are sample-aware. Ranks/deciles are partitioned by sample, performance year and attribution method. Reportable panels require >=20 members; sensitivity thresholds 10/20/30 are separate comparisons. Rank populations are selected before intersecting common providers. PostgreSQL NTILE behavior, NPI tie-breaks, missing/small population status and undefined correlations are explicit.
Medical paid = inpatient + outpatient + professional paid. EXCLUDE prescription gross cost from medical paid: it is not Medicare-paid amount. Rx gross and patient payment are separate; never add patient payment to gross. Allowed is not paid.
Preserve nonzero professional payments even if HCPCS absent (about $41.3M combined); preserve negative payments and unassigned date buckets. Do not discard conflicting segments or fan out cost by diagnoses.
Attribution A: distinct office E&M service days, then recency, then lowest NPI. B: known allowed cost over professional lines, then service days, then lowest NPI. Retain unattributed eligible member-years. Do not infer provider specialty from E&M codes.
FFS eligibility is an annual-count proxy: Part A/B 12 months, HMO 0, death-adjusted month rules. It does not establish day-level continuous enrollment.
Readmission merges overlap/same-day transfers using running maximum discharge; uses 1–30 days, discharge 2009/2010, complete observable follow-up, death/FFS exclusions, conservative invalid/conflicted episode handling. Canonical definitions are assistant/measure_definitions.md. This is not CMS risk-standardized readmission.
Risk adjustment is for COST, not readmissions: sklearn PoissonRegressor log-link, fixed alpha=1, 2008 fitting, exposure-weighted PMPM, age/sex/chronic flags. Concurrent descriptive adjustment; not causal or untouched prospective validation. Keep calibration limitations explicit.
CCS principal-condition cost is additive, any-position sensitivity is not. Avoid multiplying episode cost across diagnosis rows.
