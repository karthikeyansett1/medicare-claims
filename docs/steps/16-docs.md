## How to explain it in an interview

“The key finding is that changing attribution changes the population being measured. Between 35% and 52% of common providers moved at least three medical-cost deciles, but that does not mean their care changed. I keep each sample/year's reference population explicit, reconcile money independently, and expose the readmission exclusions. I also distinguish the working assistant implementation from unmeasured AI accuracy: the offline gold queries passed, but live evaluation remains deferred.”

Be ready to explain why medical paid excludes drug gross cost, why a 20-member panel does not guarantee a reliable readmission rate, why CCS conditions pool samples, and why concurrent cost adjustment is not prospective validation. The [canonical definitions](../../assistant/measure_definitions.md) are the detailed source.
